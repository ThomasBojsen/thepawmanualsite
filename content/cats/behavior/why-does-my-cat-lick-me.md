# Why Does My Cat Lick Me? You've Been Accepted Into the Colony

**When your cat licks you, it's allogrooming, the social grooming bonded cats exchange, and it means you've been filed as family.** In cat colonies, mutual grooming maintains relationships and builds a shared group scent; licking you is your cat doing colony maintenance on its strangest, largest member. Secondary reasons exist (you taste salty, licking gets attention, and grooming self-soothes), but the headline is affiliative: this is membership, granted. The sandpaper delivery is just how cat tongues come.

## Why it feels like sandpaper

The tongue is covered in backward-facing keratin hooks (papillae): a professional grooming tool that detangles fur and strips meat from bones, now applied to your forearm. The scratchiness isn't aggression; it's hardware. Short sessions feel affectionate; committed grooming projects on bare skin genuinely rasp. Redirecting after a minute is diplomacy, not rejection.

## The reasons, ranked

1. **Allogrooming/bonding**: the main event, often aimed at hair and head (where cats groom each other), frequently with purring.
2. **Group scent maintenance**: licking blends scents into a family signature; you're being labeled *ours*, cousin behavior to [kneading's scent-marking](/cats/behavior/why-does-my-cat-knead/).
3. **Taste**: salt from skin, lotion, food traces. Post-gym licking is usually chemistry, not sentiment.
4. **Attention economics**: if licking reliably makes you talk, pet, or laugh, it enters the toolkit ([the same ledger as meowing](/cats/behavior/why-does-my-cat-meow-so-much/)).
5. **Self-soothing**: grooming calms cats; some deploy it on you under mild stress.

**The lick-then-bite pivot:** grooming that ends in a sudden nip is usually overstimulation (the session crossed a threshold, [the full decode](/cats/behavior/why-does-my-cat-bite-me/)) or an ordinary grooming-nip, which cats also give each other. Watch tail and skin; the warning precedes the teeth.

## When does licking become a warning sign?

Three patterns flag licking as more than affection: a sudden increase in how much your cat licks you (often a stressor or something medical), overgrooming of the cat's own fur (usually allergies or parasites, not vanity), and licking or eating odd surfaces like plastic, walls, or fabric (pica, which can mean nausea, GI trouble, or a swallowing risk).

- **Sudden increase in licking you**: comfort behavior deployed at volume suggests a stressor ([territory audit](/cats/behavior/)) or, per the standing rule, [something medical](/cats/health/symptoms/).
- **Overgrooming *themselves***: bald bellies, thinning stripes, scabby skin: that's [itching until proven otherwise](/cats/health/allergies/) (allergies, parasites), not vanity, the most misread symptom in cat ownership.
- **Licking plastic, walls, fabric, or air**: pica-pattern licking flags nausea, GI trouble, or compulsive behavior: a [vet conversation](/cats/health/signs-your-cat-needs-a-vet/), and if fabric is being *eaten*, a [swallowed-material risk](/cats/health/signs-your-cat-needs-a-vet/) too.

If you'd simply like less of it: redirect gently (a stroke, a toy), never [punish a bonding behavior](/cats/behavior/), and know that offering your hand for a brief groom, then moving on, satisfies most cats' colony bookkeeping.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my cat licking me a sign of affection?

Yes: it's allogrooming, the social grooming bonded cats exchange, and being included means you're classified as family. Taste and attention play supporting roles, but the headline is membership.

### Why does my cat's tongue feel like sandpaper?

Backward-facing keratin hooks (papillae) cover the tongue: a grooming and eating tool that detangles fur and rasps meat. The scratch is hardware, not attitude.

### Why does my cat lick me and then bite me?

Either overstimulation (the session crossed a threshold; watch for tail flicks and skin ripples) or a normal grooming-nip cats also give each other. Pause at the warning signs; the [bite guide](/cats/behavior/why-does-my-cat-bite-me/) sorts the types.

### Why does my cat lick plastic bags or walls?

Odd-surface licking can be texture play, but persistent pica-pattern licking flags nausea, GI disease, or compulsive behavior: worth a [vet conversation](/cats/health/signs-your-cat-needs-a-vet/), especially if anything is being swallowed.
