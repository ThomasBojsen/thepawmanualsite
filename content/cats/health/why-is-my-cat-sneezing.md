# Why Is My Cat Sneezing? From Dust to URI, Sorted

An occasional sneeze is a nose doing its job: dust, litter plume, your candle. **Sneezing that arrives in fits, brings discharge, or comes with a subdued cat is different:** the leading cause is an upper respiratory infection (URI, "cat flu," usually feline herpesvirus or calicivirus), the same complex that runs through shelters and multi-cat homes. Most adult URIs resolve like a human cold; the exceptions that need a vet are specific and listed below, and **kittens play by harsher rules.**

## The cause lineup

Sneezing usually comes down to one of four things: an upper respiratory infection (the most common), an irritant like dust or litter, less-common allergic rhinitis, or one-sided signs that flag something more serious.

1. **Upper respiratory infection (most common):** sneezing fits, clear-then-colored nasal discharge, watery or goopy eyes, maybe mild fever and reduced pep. Herpesvirus and calicivirus cause the bulk; both spread cat-to-cat (new shelter adoptee sneezing within a week = textbook). Herpes adds a lifelong wrinkle: the virus goes latent and **flares under stress**: the cat that sneezes for a week after every move or houseguest season isn't reinfected, it's flaring.
2. **Irritants:** dusty or scented [litter](/cats/products/best-cat-litter/) (the classic: watch for sneezing right after box use), candles, diffusers and essential oils (some are [toxic to cats](/cats/health/allergies/) beyond the sneezing), smoke, cleaning sprays, renovation dust.
3. **Allergic rhinitis:** less common in cats than assumed: feline allergies mostly [go to skin](/cats/health/allergies/), but seasonal sneeze-plus-clear-discharge patterns exist.
4. **The one-nostril flags:** discharge or bleeding from **one side**, sneezing with facial swelling, or a senior with progressive one-sided signs: think foreign body (grass blade), **dental root abscess** ([teeth and noses are neighbors](/cats/health/dental-care/)), polyps, or worse. One-sided = vet, not wait-and-see.

## Home support for a mild URI

For an eating, alert adult with cold signs: **steam** (bathroom during your shower, 10–15 minutes), **wipe** eyes/nose with warm damp cotton, **feed by smell** (congestion kills appetite, so warm [wet food](/cats/food/wet-cat-food/) to raise the aroma, because the [24-hour no-food rule](/cats/food/) applies doubly to a sick cat), **hydrate** (fountains, tuna water), rest, and isolate from other cats where practical. Skip: human cold medicine in any form (multiple ingredients are toxic to cats), and essential-oil "remedies."

## When sneezing needs the vet

Kittens need a much lower threshold for a vet visit than adults, and an adult cat with labored breathing, heavy discharge, eye changes, appetite loss, or sneezing that lasts more than ten days should be seen too.

- **Kittens:** low threshold, period: URIs dehydrate and starve small bodies fast, and eye infections can scar; a sneezy kitten that's also quiet or not eating goes [same-day](/cats/health/signs-your-cat-needs-a-vet/). (Vaccination [reduces severity](/cats/kitten-care/kitten-vaccination-schedule/): the FVRCP core covers herpes and calici.)
- **Any cat:** not eating, lethargy, **labored or open-mouth breathing (emergency)**, thick green/yellow discharge, eye squinting or ulcers, symptoms beyond ~10 days, or worsening after initial improvement (secondary bacterial infections are the common plot twist, treatable, but diagnosed, not guessed).
- **The pattern cases:** recurrent flares (herpes management options exist, a vet conversation), and chronic year-round sneezing (workup territory).

> **Veterinary disclaimer:** This guide is educational. Kittens with URI signs, any cat with breathing difficulty, eye changes, or appetite loss, and symptoms beyond ten days need a licensed veterinarian.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my cat sneezing so much all of a sudden?

Sudden fits point at either an irritant (new litter, candle, cleaning spray, check what changed) or the start of an upper respiratory infection, especially in a recently adopted or multi-cat-exposed cat. Watch appetite and energy; they decide the urgency.

### Do cat colds go away on their own?

Mild URIs in healthy adults typically resolve in 7–10 days with supportive care, like a human cold. Not eating, lethargy, breathing effort, eye changes, or symptoms past ten days move it from "cold" to "vet visit."

### Can I give my cat anything for sneezing?

No human cold medicine: several common ingredients are toxic to cats. Safe support is steam sessions, warmed wet food, hydration, and rest; anything pharmaceutical comes from the vet.

### Why does my cat sneeze after using the litter box?

Litter dust: fine, scented, or clay-heavy litters launch a plume with every dig. Switching to a low-dust, unscented litter usually ends the post-box sneezing ([litter guide](/cats/products/best-cat-litter/)).
