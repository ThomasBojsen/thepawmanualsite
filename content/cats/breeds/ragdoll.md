# Ragdoll: The Cat That Chose People

The Ragdoll is the cat for people who want a *companion*, not a roommate: bred specifically for docility and human attachment, many go characteristically limp when picked up (the namesake), follow their person between rooms, and greet at the door. Among pedigreed cats it has topped registration statistics in recent years — deservedly, with two owner obligations attached: **strictly indoor life** (this much trust has no street survival value) and **HCM-screened breeding lines only.**

## Ragdoll quick facts

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size | Large — males 15–20 lb, females 10–15 lb; slow maturing (3–4 years) |
| Lifespan | 12–17 years |
| Coat | Semi-long, silky, minimal undercoat; brushing ~2× weekly |
| Eyes/pattern | Blue eyes, pointed patterns (colorpoint/mitted/bicolor) |
| Voice | Quiet, polite |
| Energy | Low–moderate; a floor cat more than a climber |
| The catch | Velcro attachment — long-empty homes are hard on them |

## Temperament: velcro, floppy, floor-level

Ragdolls run people-first to a degree unusual even among social breeds — lap time, doorway greetings, and a tolerance for handling that makes them famous with gentle children ([family list](/cats/breeds/family-cats/)). Two structural notes: they're **floor cats** (less vertical drive than most — low, stable [perches beat towers](/cats/products/best-cat-trees/)), and their trusting, low-defense temperament is precisely why the breed standard-bearers insist on **indoor-only** — a cat that doesn't flee threats needs walls between it and the world. That same attachment means all-day-empty homes suit them poorly; a bonded pair is a common and good solution.

## Living with a Ragdoll

Enrichment is gentle-grade: wand-toy [hunting sessions](/cats/products/best-cat-toys/) (they play lying down more than most — normal), puzzle feeders, and window real estate. Watch the weight from year one — low energy plus large frame plus food-motivated is the [obesity](/cats/food/how-much-should-i-feed-my-cat/) recipe, and an estimated 61% of U.S. cats are already overweight (APOP). The [monthly weigh-in habit](/cats/health/) earns its keep here.

## Grooming

Blessedly easy for the length: the silky, low-undercoat coat resists matting — brushing twice weekly covers it, a bit more in spring. Check the britches and armpits, where what mats does mat. Occasional [hairballs](/cats/health/hairballs/) at this coat length are normal; frequent means something.

## Health

**HCM (hypertrophic cardiomyopathy)** is the breed's serious one — a Ragdoll-specific mutation (MyBPC3 variant) is genetically testable, so "our lines are tested + echo-screened" is the minimum breeder answer, not a bonus. Also noted in the breed: **bladder stones/urinary issues** (another argument for [wet-food hydration](/cats/food/wet-cat-food/) — and every owner of a male cat memorizes the [straining-in-the-box emergency](/cats/health/signs-your-cat-needs-a-vet/)) and the weight tendency above. Well-bred, well-kept Ragdolls are long-lived companions.

> **Veterinary disclaimer:** This profile is general breed information, not veterinary advice. Health decisions for an individual cat belong with a licensed veterinarian.

## Is a Ragdoll right for you?

**Yes, if you:** want maximum companionship per cat; are home a fair amount (or will get two); have gentle kids; accept indoor-only as policy.

**Look elsewhere if you:** want independence ([British Shorthair](/cats/breeds/british-shorthair/)); want an athlete ([Bengal](/cats/breeds/bengal/)); or want a cat for an empty-all-day one-cat home.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Ragdolls really go limp when you hold them?

Many do — the relaxed flop is the breed's namesake trait, though individuals vary from full-noodle to merely tolerant. Either way, handling tolerance runs far above cat-average.

### Can Ragdolls be left alone all day?

They cope worse than most breeds — Ragdolls are attachment-first cats, and chronic solitude shows up as stress behaviors. If your schedule is long, adopt a bonded pair or reconsider the breed.

### Why do Ragdolls have to stay indoors?

Their docile, low-flight temperament removes the survival instincts outdoor life demands — the breed community and rescues near-universally require indoor homes. Enrichment ([play, perches, territory](/cats/behavior/)) replaces the outdoors.

### Are Ragdolls healthy?

Generally long-lived (12–17 years) *when* bred responsibly — HCM is the screening non-negotiable (genetic test + echocardiograms in breeding cats), with weight and urinary health as the ownable factors at home.
