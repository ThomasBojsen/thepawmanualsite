# Persian: Serenity, Purchased With a Comb

The Persian is the calmest, quietest, most ornamental cat in the mainstream breeds — a serene, sweet-tempered companion that prefers predictable homes, low perches, and scheduled affection. The purchase price is paid daily, in grooming: that magnificent coat mats *aggressively* without **daily** combing, and the flat face that defines the modern show Persian brings the feline version of the [French Bulldog conversation](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/) — tear-duct overflow, breathing limits, and eye vulnerability. This profile gives you the real daily routine before the beautiful photos make the decision for you.

## Persian quick facts

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size | Medium — 7–12 lb, low-slung and sturdy |
| Lifespan | 12–17 years (well-bred, well-kept) |
| Coat | Long, dense undercoat — **daily combing, non-negotiable** |
| Face | Flat (brachycephalic) in show lines; "doll-face" lines are less extreme |
| Voice | Quiet, infrequent |
| Energy | Low — a floor-and-sofa cat, not a climber |
| Best home | Calm, predictable, grooming-committed |

## Temperament: the low-drama companion

Persians are gentle, quiet, and affectionate in an undemanding register — happy to share a room, nap decoratively, and accept petting sessions on schedule. They dislike chaos: boisterous households, ambush-grade toddlers, and in-your-face dogs stress them ([calmer family picks](/cats/breeds/family-cats/) exist if that's your house). Play happens in short, dignified bursts ([wand sessions](/cats/products/best-cat-toys/) still matter — weight creeps on low-energy breeds; [feeding math here](/cats/food/how-much-should-i-feed-my-cat/)).

## The grooming contract (read twice)

- **Daily:** full-body comb-through with a metal comb — the undercoat mats at the skin where brushes glide over; armpits, britches, and belly first. Ten minutes a day; skipping a week can mean a shave-down under sedation.
- **Daily:** eye-area wiping — the flat face overflows tear ducts, and unmanaged tear staining macerates the skin beneath.
- **Regularly:** sanitary trims (long coat + litter box = physics), occasional baths done properly (thorough drying — damp undercoat mats *and* chills), and nail trims.
- **Litter logistics:** fine litter clings to long coat — [box and litter choice](/cats/products/best-cat-litter/) matters more for Persians than for any shorthair.

If daily doesn't fit your life, the honest alternatives are a professional-groom schedule (lion cuts are functional, not shameful) — or a [different breed](/cats/breeds/british-shorthair/).

## Health: two screening conversations

1. **PKD (polycystic kidney disease)** — historically widespread in Persian lines; a genetic test exists, so "PKD-negative parents, in writing" is the minimum breeder standard. No test documentation, no purchase.
2. **Brachycephalic anatomy** — the flat face brings noisy breathing, heat sensitivity (treat hot days [like a Frenchie owner does](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/)), dental crowding ([dental care](/cats/health/dental-care/) pays extra), and vulnerable, protruding eyes — corneal scratches are never wait-and-see ([vet signs](/cats/health/signs-your-cat-needs-a-vet/)). "Doll-face" Persians (the older, less extreme face) trade show ring for airway, a trade worth considering.

Also monitor: [hairballs](/cats/health/hairballs/) (long coat = higher baseline, but frequent still isn't normal) and weight.

> **Veterinary disclaimer:** This profile is general breed information, not veterinary advice. Health decisions for an individual cat belong with a licensed veterinarian.

## Is a Persian right for you?

**Yes, if you:** run a calm, predictable home; genuinely accept the daily grooming ritual (some owners love it — it's the bonding hour); want quiet, low-energy companionship; will insist on PKD-tested lines.

**Look elsewhere if you:** can't commit daily; run a loud, busy, toddler-forward household; want a playful athlete ([Bengal](/cats/breeds/bengal/)) or an easy-keeper ([British Shorthair](/cats/breeds/british-shorthair/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much grooming does a Persian cat need?

Daily — a full comb-through with a metal comb plus eye-area cleaning, ten-ish minutes, every day. The undercoat mats at the skin fast enough that a skipped week can end in sedated shave-downs.

### Are Persian cats healthy?

Well-bred ones are reasonably robust, but two issues define the breed: PKD (demand genetic testing of parents) and flat-face anatomy — breathing, eye, dental, and heat limitations. "Doll-face" lines moderate the second.

### Are Persians good with kids and dogs?

With calm kids and mellow pets, yes — they're patient and unaggressive. Boisterous households stress them; a Persian's ideal home is predictable, quiet, and ambush-free.

### Why do Persian cats' eyes water?

The flattened face compresses tear drainage, so tears overflow onto the face — daily wiping prevents staining and skin irritation. *Increased* discharge, squinting, or color change is an eye problem, not a quirk — [vet promptly](/cats/health/signs-your-cat-needs-a-vet/).
