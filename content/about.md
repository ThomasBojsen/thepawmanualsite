# About ThePawManual

ThePawManual exists because pet advice online is a mess: contradictory, outdated, and optimized for clicks over correctness. We publish free, research-backed guides and interactive tools for dog and cat owners — every health claim sourced to veterinary authorities, every limitation stated plainly, and every page honest about when a question belongs at the vet's office instead of a search result.

## What we make

- **Guides** on [dog care](/dogs/) and [cat care](/cats/) — breeds, food, health, training, and behavior — written to answer the question you actually searched, sourced to organizations like the American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA), AAHA, and AAFCO. How we research and source is documented in our [editorial process](/editorial-process/).
- **Free tools** — [calculators and selectors](/tools/) that turn veterinary guidelines into instant, personalized answers. No signup, no paywall; the math runs in your browser.

## How we make money

Two ways, both disclosed: display advertising, and affiliate commissions when you buy through links in our [product guides](/dogs/products/) — detailed in our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/). What we don't do: sponsored rankings, pay-to-play recommendations, or advice shaped by what earns. If a cheap option is the right option, we say so.

## Our promises

1. **Sourced, not vibes.** Health and nutrition claims cite veterinary authorities, linked on the page.
2. **Honest limits.** We're an educational resource, not your veterinarian — every health page says so, and means it.
3. **Kept current.** Guides carry visible dates and get reviewed on a schedule ([how](/editorial-process/)).
4. **Corrections welcomed.** Spot an error? [Tell us](/contact/) — we'd rather fix it than defend it.
