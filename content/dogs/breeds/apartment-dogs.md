# Best Apartment Dogs: 10 Breeds That Genuinely Thrive in Small Spaces

A good apartment dog isn't defined by size; it's defined by three things: calm behavior indoors, a low barking baseline, and exercise needs you can meet with walks instead of a yard. That's why a 70 lb Greyhound is a better apartment dog than a 20 lb Beagle, and why the [French Bulldog](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/) tops this list while several small terriers don't make it at all.

## The 10 best apartment breeds at a glance

The French Bulldog, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, and retired Greyhound top the list for calm indoor behavior and minimal barking, proving that apartment fit depends on temperament, not size.

## The two picks that surprise people

- **Greyhound:** the 45-mph couch potato. Retired racers sleep 18 hours a day, rarely bark, and need a couple of walks plus an occasional sprint in a fenced area. Big dog, small footprint: the main furniture requirement is a [proper bed](/dogs/products/best-dog-beds/) for their bony frames.
- **Basenji:** the "barkless" dog: it genuinely can't bark (it yodels occasionally). The trade: terrier-grade intelligence and independence, so [training](/dogs/training/puppy-training/) and enrichment are mandatory.

## Which dog breeds struggle in apartments?

**Beagles, Huskies, Border Collies, and young Labs or Goldens are the popular breeds that most often struggle in apartments.** Beagles howl when lonely (a lease-ender), Huskies have exercise needs no apartment schedule meets, Border Collies and most working breeds turn under-stimulation into destruction, and young [Labs](/dogs/breeds/labrador-retriever/)/[Goldens](/dogs/breeds/golden-retriever/) *can* work but only with serious daily exercise commitment.

If your heart is set on one anyway, be realistic about the [barking](/dogs/training/how-to-stop-dog-barking/) and [separation anxiety](/dogs/training/separation-anxiety-in-dogs/) risks: your neighbors are stakeholders in this decision.

## Making any apartment work

Making an apartment work for any dog comes down to four things: proactive noise training, a consistent potty routine, honest alone-time limits, and checking your lease's breed and weight restrictions before you commit.

1. **Noise management is training, not luck:** teach a quiet cue early ([barking guide](/dogs/training/how-to-stop-dog-barking/)) and use enrichment ([chew toys that last](/dogs/products/best-dog-toys-for-chewers/), puzzle feeders) for the hours you're out.
2. **Potty logistics:** a [consistent schedule](/dogs/training/potty-training/) beats a balcony patch; elevator buildings add minutes: factor it into puppy [potty-training](/dogs/training/potty-training/) frequency.
3. **The alone-time question** decides more than square footage: dogs bred for companionship (Havanese, Cavalier) struggle with 9-hour absences anywhere, mansion or studio.
4. **Check the lease** for breed and weight restrictions before you fall in love. Renting with pets? See [pet costs](/pet-guides/pet-costs/) for deposits and pet rent.

Torn between two? The [dog breed selector](/tools/dog-breed-selector/) weighs your building, schedule, and tolerance for hair. See also [small breeds](/dogs/breeds/small-dogs/) and [low-shedding picks](/dogs/breeds/low-shedding-dogs/) for adjacent lists.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best dog for apartment living?

The [French Bulldog](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/) is the strongest all-around pick: small, quiet, low-exercise, and content indoors. The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and retired Greyhound are close behind, and the Greyhound proves size isn't the deciding factor.

### Can big dogs live in apartments?

Yes: calm, low-energy large breeds like Greyhounds do better in apartments than many small, vocal, high-energy breeds. What matters is indoor calm, barking level, and whether walks can cover the exercise need.

### How much exercise does an apartment dog need?

The breeds on this list need 20–60 minutes daily, delivered as walks and play, no yard required. The honest math: a yard never exercised a dog anyway; walks and engagement do, whatever your housing.

### Which dogs bark the least?

The Basenji (physically can't bark), Greyhound, [French Bulldog](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/), and Cavalier are among the quietest. No dog is silent, but baseline temperament plus [early training](/dogs/training/puppy-training/) keeps noise complaints theoretical.
