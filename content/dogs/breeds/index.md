# Dog Breed Guides: Find the Right Dog for Your Life

The American Kennel Club recognizes about 200 dog breeds, but the right one for you comes down to five factors: size, energy level, grooming needs, tolerance for being alone, and fit with kids or other pets. Our breed profiles rate every dog on exactly those factors — and our lifestyle lists work backwards from your situation instead of forwards from a photo. The French Bulldog has topped AKC registration statistics since 2022, but popularity is a poor reason to choose a dog; fit is everything.

## How to choose: the five factors that matter

1. **Size** — affects food costs, vet costs, housing rules, and physical handling. See [what a dog costs](/pet-guides/pet-costs/).
2. **Energy** — an under-exercised high-energy dog becomes a [barking](/dogs/training/how-to-stop-dog-barking/), chewing, digging dog.
3. **Grooming and shedding** — daily brushing is a lifestyle commitment, not a chore you'll "get to."
4. **Alone-time tolerance** — full-time office job? Some breeds handle it; others develop [separation anxiety](/dogs/training/separation-anxiety-in-dogs/).
5. **Household fit** — kids, cats, other dogs, elderly family members. Temperament beats looks every time.

Once you've chosen, start with the [new puppy checklist](/dogs/puppy-care/new-puppy-checklist/) and the [puppy food guide](/dogs/food/puppy-food/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the most popular dog breed in the U.S.?

The French Bulldog has been the most popular breed by AKC registrations since 2022, ending the Labrador Retriever's 31-year run at the top. Read our honest [French Bulldog profile](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/) — including the breathing and health issues that come with the breed's popularity.

### What is the best dog breed for first-time owners?

Breeds that forgive training mistakes and don't demand expert handling: the [Golden Retriever](/dogs/breeds/golden-retriever/), [Labrador Retriever](/dogs/breeds/labrador-retriever/), and [Poodle](/dogs/breeds/poodle/) top most first-timer lists. Our [family dogs guide](/dogs/breeds/family-dogs/) ranks them side by side.

### Which dog breeds shed the least?

Poodles, Bichon Frises, Portuguese Water Dogs, and several terriers shed minimally — though no dog is truly hypoallergenic. The full ranking is in our [low-shedding dogs guide](/dogs/breeds/low-shedding-dogs/).

### Should I choose a breed by temperament or by looks?

Temperament, always. Coat color won't walk itself at 6 a.m. in January. Use the five-factor checklist above, or the [dog breed selector](/tools/dog-breed-selector/) if you want a shortlist in under a minute.
