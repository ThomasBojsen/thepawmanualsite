# Dog Training That Works: Kind, Consistent, Evidence-Based

Modern dog training is built on positive reinforcement — rewarding the behavior you want instead of punishing the behavior you don't. It's the approach recommended by the American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior (AVSAB), it builds trust instead of fear, and it works on everything from an 8-week-old puppy to an adult rescue with baggage. Every guide below uses it; none of them will tell you to buy a shock collar.

## Puppy fundamentals

Training starts the day the puppy comes home — not at six months, as the old advice went. [Puppy training](/dogs/training/puppy-training/) covers name response, sit, handling, and socialization; [potty training](/dogs/training/potty-training/) and [crate training](/dogs/training/crate-training/) run in parallel from day one. Pair them with the [8-week-old puppy schedule](/dogs/puppy-care/8-week-old-puppy-schedule/) so training fits the puppy's actual sleep-play-eat rhythm.

## Everyday skills

[Leash training](/dogs/training/leash-training/) is the highest-return skill you can teach: a dog that walks politely gets more walks, more outings, and a better life. The right gear helps — see our [harness buying guide](/dogs/products/best-dog-harnesses/) for why front-clip harnesses beat collars for pullers.

## Problem behaviors

[Barking](/dogs/training/how-to-stop-dog-barking/) and [separation anxiety](/dogs/training/separation-anxiety-in-dogs/) are the two problems that most often push owners to give up a dog — and both are fixable with a structured plan. Note that separation anxiety is a panic response, not disobedience; severe cases warrant a veterinary behaviorist, and sudden behavior changes in a previously calm dog can signal [a health problem](/dogs/health/signs-your-dog-needs-a-vet/). For the "why" behind quirky-but-normal behaviors, see the [dog behavior section](/dogs/behavior/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to potty train a puppy?

Most puppies are reliably potty trained in 4–8 weeks with a consistent schedule, though some take several months. The keys are frequent trips out, rewards within two seconds of success, and zero punishment for accidents. Full method in our [potty training guide](/dogs/training/potty-training/).

### When should I start training my puppy?

Immediately — at 8 weeks a puppy can learn name response, sit, and gentle handling in 2–3 minute sessions. The prime socialization window closes around 12–14 weeks, so early, positive exposure to people, sounds, and surfaces matters more than any command. See [puppy training](/dogs/training/puppy-training/).

### Is crate training cruel?

No — done right, a crate works with a dog's denning instinct and becomes a voluntary safe place, not a cage. Cruel is the misuse: too many hours, or using it as punishment. Our [crate training guide](/dogs/training/crate-training/) covers duration limits by age.

### Do shock collars work for training?

Aversive tools can suppress behavior short-term, but studies and the AVSAB position statement link them to increased fear, anxiety, and aggression — and they don't teach the dog what to do instead. Positive reinforcement gets better results without the fallout.
