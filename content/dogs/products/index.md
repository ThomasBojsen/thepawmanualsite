Most dog gear gets bought twice: the cheap version first, then the right one after it fails. Our buying guides exist to skip that first purchase. ## How we pick (and what we skip)

- **Specs before brands.** A crate's gauge and door latches matter; the brand's Instagram doesn't.
- **Sized recommendations.** A pick that's right for a [French Bulldog](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/) is wrong for a [German Shepherd](/dogs/breeds/german-shepherd/): every guide recommends by size and use case.
- **Durability weighting.** Especially for chewers: replacement cost is the real price. Our [chew-toy guide](/dogs/products/best-dog-toys-for-chewers/) is written for the dogs that destroy "indestructible."
- **No pay-to-play.** Commissions never decide a ranking; our [editorial process](/editorial-process/) explains how recommendations are made and updated.

Buying for a new puppy? The [new puppy checklist](/dogs/puppy-care/new-puppy-checklist/) tells you what you need on day one, and what can wait a month.

## Frequently asked questions

### What size dog crate should I buy?

Big enough for your dog to stand, turn around, and lie down fully stretched, and no bigger for potty-training puppies (buy adult size with a divider). Exact measuring instructions are in the [crate guide](/dogs/products/best-dog-crates/).

### Is a harness better than a collar?

For walks, usually yes: a front-clip harness gives you control over pullers without pressure on the throat, and it's essential for flat-faced breeds like [French Bulldogs](/dogs/breeds/french-bulldog/). Keep a collar for the ID tag. Details in the [harness guide](/dogs/products/best-dog-harnesses/).

### Are GPS dog trackers worth it?

If your dog has ever escaped once, yes: a subscription tracker costs less per year than one "lost dog" afternoon. What to check: battery life, cellular coverage, and subscription cost. Compared in the [GPS tracker guide](/dogs/products/best-gps-dog-trackers/).

### What dog toys are safe for aggressive chewers?

Solid natural rubber (Kong-style), nylon chews sized up, and nothing with squeakers left unsupervised. Tennis balls wear down teeth over time. Our [chew-toy guide](/dogs/products/best-dog-toys-for-chewers/) ranks toys by destruction resistance.
