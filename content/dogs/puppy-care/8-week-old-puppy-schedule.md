# 8-Week-Old Puppy Schedule: What a Day Actually Looks Like

The number that surprises every new owner: an 8-week-old puppy sleeps **18–20 hours a day**. Most "crazy puppy" problems are actually overtired-puppy problems. The day runs in repeating 2–3 hour loops of *wake → potty → eat or play → potty → nap*, with four meals, a potty trip every hour while awake, and training folded into play in two-minute bursts. Here's a realistic template, why enforced naps are the secret ingredient, and how the schedule evolves.

## What does an 8-week-old puppy's daily schedule look like?

A typical day breaks into repeating 2–3 hour loops of wake, potty, eat or play, potty, and nap: four meals, a potty trip every hour while awake, and training folded into two-minute play bursts.

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | Wake → straight [outside to potty](/dogs/training/potty-training/) → **meal 1** → potty again |
| 7:30–8:15 | Play + 2-minute [training rep](/dogs/training/puppy-training/) → potty → nap in [crate](/dogs/training/crate-training/) |
| 10:00 | Wake → potty → play/handling practice → potty |
| 11:00 | **Meal 2** → potty → nap |
| 13:00 | Wake → potty → socialization mini-outing or garden play → potty → nap |
| 15:00 | **Meal 3** → potty → play → potty → nap |
| 17:30 | Wake → potty → family time (supervised floor freedom) |
| 19:00 | **Meal 4** (last food of the day) → potty |
| 19:30–21:00 | Calm play, chew-toy wind-down: expect the "witching hour" (below) |
| 21:30 | Water up, last potty trip, lights out: [first-night setup here](/dogs/puppy-care/first-night-with-puppy/) |
| Overnight | 1–2 businesslike potty trips (bladder holds 2–3 hours at this age) |

A template, not a law: shift the anchors to your life, keep the *loop structure* and the meal count. Portions for those four meals: the [puppy food guide](/dogs/food/puppy-food/).

## Enforced naps: the counterintuitive fix

Puppies, like toddlers, don't self-regulate sleep; they escalate instead. The overtired signature: sudden frantic zoomies, piranha-grade [biting](/dogs/puppy-care/puppy-teething-guide/) that redirect can't touch, and an inability to settle. The fix isn't more play; it's the crate, a chew, and twenty dark minutes, asleep before the whining finishes. If your 7 p.m. puppy turns into a landshark every evening, that's the "witching hour," and it's a nap deficit wearing a costume. Guard the 18–20 hours deliberately.

## Play, training, and the outside world

Awake blocks of 30–60 minutes are plenty, with play in 10–15 minute bursts. Skip forced walks entirely at 8 weeks: garden and living-room play covers exercise, and joints in [large breeds](/dogs/food/puppy-food/) prefer it that way. What *does* belong in every day: handling practice (paws, ears, mouth), name games, and carried [socialization](/dogs/training/puppy-training/) outings; the world-viewing matters weeks before the world-walking does, and the [vaccine schedule](/dogs/puppy-care/puppy-vaccination-schedule/) governs the ground rules.

## How does a puppy's schedule change as it grows?

The schedule loosens with age: potty trips stretch toward every 2 hours by 10–12 weeks, meals drop from four to three at 12–16 weeks, and settle at two meals a day by 6 months as the nap total drifts down toward adult levels.

- **10–12 weeks:** potty trips stretch toward every 2 hours; awake blocks lengthen; overnight often drops to one trip.
- **12–16 weeks:** meals go 4 → 3; first real leash outings post-vaccines; many puppies [sleep through the night](/dogs/puppy-care/first-night-with-puppy/).
- **6 months:** meals 3 → 2, the adult rhythm; potty every 4–6 hours; the nap total drifts down toward adult levels.

The loop logic never changes (potty after waking, eating, and playing); it just runs on longer cycles. When the schedule suddenly *stops* working (accidents return, meals refused), don't tighten the timetable; [check for a medical reason](/dogs/health/signs-your-dog-needs-a-vet/): puppies have thin margins.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do 8-week-old puppies sleep?

18–20 hours per day, and they need every one: most "hyper" or bitey evening behavior is overtiredness, fixed by an enforced crate nap rather than more play.

### How often does an 8-week-old puppy need to pee?

Roughly every hour while awake, plus immediately after waking, eating, and playing; and every 2–3 hours overnight. The [potty training guide](/dogs/training/potty-training/) has the full age chart.

### How long should I play with my 8-week-old puppy?

Bursts of 10–15 minutes within 30–60 minute awake windows, then a nap. Skip structured walks for now: free play covers exercise without loading growing joints.

### Why does my puppy go crazy in the evening?

The "witching hour" is an overtired puppy escalating: zoomies and intense biting are fatigue, not defiance. Front-load an extra afternoon nap and move the evening wind-down earlier.
