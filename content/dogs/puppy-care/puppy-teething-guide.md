# Puppy Teething: The Timeline, the Relief, and the Furniture Insurance

Puppies lose 28 baby teeth and grow 42 adult ones, with the messy middle running from about **12 weeks to 6 months** — peak chewing chaos lands at 12–20 weeks, driven by genuinely sore gums. Finding tiny teeth on the floor is normal; finding none is also normal (puppies swallow them, harmlessly). Your job during teething: appropriate things to chew, cold for the sore gums, patient redirection for everything else — and one check at the end that the baby teeth actually left.

## The timeline

| Age | What's happening in there |
|---|---|
| 2–4 weeks | Baby teeth erupt (breeder-side; nursing gets spicy) |
| 8–12 weeks | Full set of 28 baby teeth — the needle-tip era your hands remember |
| 12–16 weeks | Incisors fall; adult incisors arrive — chewing urge climbs |
| 4–6 months | Canines, premolars, molars turn over — **peak teething** |
| 6–7 months | All 42 adult teeth in; the storm passes |

Blood spots on toys, briefly tender mouth, and a day of picky eating are all within normal. What's not: see the red flags below.

## Relief that works (and is safe)

- **Cold is the medicine:** a wet washcloth, knotted and frozen, is the classic; frozen rubber chews and chilled (not rock-hard-frozen) Kong-style toys ditto. Frozen carrot chunks work for supervised chewers — count the calories in the [treat budget](/dogs/food/how-much-should-i-feed-my-dog/).
- **Texture variety, rotated:** several chew types, a few out at a time, swapped to stay novel — picks in the [chew-toy guide](/dogs/products/best-dog-toys-for-chewers/).
- **The hardness rule from day one:** antlers, hooves, and hard nylon **break teeth** — adult *and* baby. The vet "kneecap rule" (too hard for your knee = too hard for teeth) applies at every age; details in the [dental guide](/dogs/health/dental-care/).
- **Skip:** human teething gels (some ingredients are unsafe for dogs), ice cubes in quantity for tiny mouths, and anything small enough to swallow.

## Hands and furniture: the redirect economy

Teething turns everything into a chew candidate — the fix is making the *right* candidates win. Teeth on skin: the [yelp-pause-redirect routine](/dogs/training/puppy-training/) from puppy training, four hundred repetitions, fades with the baby teeth. Furniture: management first (gate the good room, [crate](/dogs/training/crate-training/) with a chew when unsupervised), a stocked chew supply within puppy reach always, and honest expectations about bitter sprays — they help some puppies, amuse others. And remember the [overtiredness rule](/dogs/puppy-care/8-week-old-puppy-schedule/): the worst biting hours are usually nap-deficit hours, not teething at all.

## Red flags and the 6-month check

Call the vet for: a **broken or cracked tooth**, refusal to eat beyond a day ([small margins](/dogs/health/signs-your-dog-needs-a-vet/)), significant bleeding, or facial swelling. And book the one check most owners skip: at ~6 months (often bundled with spay/neuter), have the vet confirm no **retained baby teeth** — a stubborn baby canine sitting beside its adult replacement crowds the mouth and traps debris, and it's especially common in [small breeds](/dogs/breeds/small-dogs/). Retained teeth get extracted; ignored, they become [dental disease](/dogs/health/dental-care/) with a head start.

Teething ends; teeth stay. Use the mouth-handling tolerance you're building now to start the [brushing habit](/dogs/health/dental-care/) — a 6-month-old that accepts a toothbrush is a gift to your future self and your vet bill.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does puppy teething last?

From roughly 12 weeks to 6 months, with the worst chewing at 12–20 weeks. By 6–7 months all 42 adult teeth are in and the drive drops sharply.

### When do puppies lose their baby teeth?

Incisors from about 12–16 weeks, canines and the rest through 4–6 months. Swallowed teeth are normal and harmless — most owners never find a single one.

### What helps a teething puppy?

Cold: frozen knotted washcloths, chilled rubber chews, frozen carrot pieces — plus a rotated variety of appropriate-hardness chew toys. Skip human teething gels and anything harder than your kneecap.

### Do puppies stop biting after teething?

Mostly — mouthing drops sharply once adult teeth settle and gums stop hurting, *if* the redirect training ran alongside. A post-teething adolescent that still bites hard needs [training attention](/dogs/training/puppy-training/), not more waiting.
