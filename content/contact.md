# Contact ThePawManual

One inbox for everything: questions, corrections, story ideas, and the occasional dog photo (encouraged).

## Reach us

- **Email:** [info@thepawmanual.com](mailto:info@thepawmanual.com)
- **Corrections:** spotted an error in a guide? Email with the page URL and the issue. We'll check it, fix it if it's wrong, and note the correction on the page ([how we handle it](/editorial-process/)).
- **Partnerships & press:** the same address. We're two people, not a company with a separate press line.

## Before you write: two things we can't do

1. **We can't diagnose your pet or give medical advice.** If your dog or cat is unwell, contact a licensed veterinarian, and if it's urgent, our [warning signs guide](/dogs/health/signs-your-dog-needs-a-vet/) helps you gauge how urgent. Suspected poisoning: ASPCA Animal Poison Control, (888) 426-4435.
2. **We don't accept sponsored posts or paid placements** in editorial content. Product samples are fine; coverage promises are not (see [how we work](/editorial-process/)).

*Response time: we're a two-person team, not a support desk, so there's no fixed turnaround.*
